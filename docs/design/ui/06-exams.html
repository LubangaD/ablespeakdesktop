What the student can say

Moving and hearing

“Read the question”, “read the options”, “next”, “previous”, “go to question 5”, “go to question 2 b”, “slower”, “faster”, “how much time is left”, “where am I”, “unanswered questions”, “read the instructions”, “help”, “finish exam”.

Answering

“Answer B” for a choice. “Answer” to start writing: everything said is written until “done”. “Full stop”, “comma”, “new paragraph”; “delete the last word”, “delete the last sentence”, “undo”, “read my answer”, “spell m i t o” to write a word letter by letter; “maths on” for symbols.

For practice and mock exams only.

Turning speech into text needs the internet, and the student’s voice goes to Google Gemini — outside Kenya. For a formal exam that needs parental consent and a data protection assessment, and KNEC has published no rule yet on speech-to-text. Recognition on this computer, with no audio leaving it, is the next step. See docs/AbleSpeak-Exam-Mode.md.
